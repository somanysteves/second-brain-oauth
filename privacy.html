Privacy policy

Second Brain is a personal tool. Only the account holders who sign in to it themselves use it.

  • Google user data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Chat) is accessed only to perform actions the account holder requests.
  • OAuth tokens and data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
